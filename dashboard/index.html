      How it works

      1. Watchlist. Every protocol DefiLlama tracks is screened: out go any protocol that already records a token, and any wrapped or staked product of something that trades (LSTs / LRTs such as stETH, eETH, ezETH, LBTC). Launchpads and trading bots have no TVL and are out of scope. What is left is the pre-token board.
      2. Peer multiple. For each category, the median FDV/TVL of its traded comparables (winsorised to 0.2–15× so outliers cannot dominate), capped at 5× the market-wide median. A category with fewer than 3 comps falls back to the global median.
      3. Implied FDV = TVL × category multiple × (1 + 0.25 × 7-day growth, clamped) × maturity factor (0.6–1.0 by age). The multiple already prices size, so TVL enters linearly — no hidden exponent. Growth is the snapshot's change_7d; age is months from listed_at to the snapshot date.
      4. Launch odds are a logistic prior on age, points program, venture funding and momentum. A prior, not a prediction.
      5. Radar score (0–100) = 40% log implied FDV + 25% growth + 20% launch odds + 15% farmability (points program, multi-chain, low cost). Verdicts: PRIME ≥ 45, WATCH 30–44, LONG SHOT < 30 — bands set on this snapshot's score distribution (top score 50). Farmer EV = implied FDV × launch odds × 5% (the assumed share of FDV that reaches farmers).
      6. Farm plan. Each row gets concrete steps for its category and chain, with rough costs. Regenerate everything with node main.ts — the board, the dossier and this page all come from the same computation.
